# Documentation checks and their limits

Checked 18 November 2026. The compliance command reports missing fields and overdue dates. It is a review prompt, not a compliance determination or an audit opinion.

## Sources

[ISA (NZ) 230, current compilation](https://standards.xrb.govt.nz/standards-navigator/isa-nz-230/), paragraphs 8-9, 14-16 and A21-A24, addresses audit documentation, preparation/review attribution, timely final-file assembly and retention. It describes an ordinary assembly period of no more than 60 days. Retention depends on the applicable requirements and later group report dates. The firm records its own approved dates here.

[XRB applicability page](https://www.xrb.govt.nz/standards/assurance-standards/auditing-standards/isa-nz-230/) distinguishes the version for periods beginning on or after 15 December 2023 from the new version for periods beginning on or after 15 December 2026. Select the applicable version for the engagement period, not just today's date.

## Implemented checks

- evidence_missing: a workpaper has no evidence reference.
- preparation_missing: the procedure, preparer and conclusion await recording.
- review_pending: reviewer attribution and review extent await recording.
- assembly_date_missing and assembly_overdue: the firm's recorded deadline is absent or passed.
- retention_missing and retention_review: retention needs a decision or review. Nothing is deleted.
- standard_review: the firm has not recorded its applicable standard version.

Each is a narrow record check connected to the source above. No check establishes evidence quality, completeness, applicability or legal compliance.

## Firm policy controls

The separate-reviewer rule, one approval per workpaper and blocking all open points are conservative workspace policies. They are not represented as universal legal requirements. Firms with another authorised review model can change these rules through /customise, with tests and documented approval.

Assembly locks ordinary edits. A reopening records a reason and keeps prior values in activity history. The firm must decide whether any proposed post-report or post-assembly change is permissible, and what extra documentation is required. Database-owner access can change data; use an independently protected archive where required.

AU and OTHER engagements are supported as records, but this version does not implement their jurisdiction-specific rules. Record the correct local standards and policies before relying on dates. No licensed practice aids, audit programs or professional opinions are supplied.
